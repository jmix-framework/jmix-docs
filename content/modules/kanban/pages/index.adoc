The xref:flow-ui:vc/components/kanban.adoc[kanban] component visualizes a project's workflow. It uses cards to represent tasks and columns to represent different stages, allowing users to see progress at a glance. The add-on is based on the https://htmlelements.com/demos/kanban/overview/[Smart Kanban^] JavaScript library.

image::overview-embedded-kanban.png[align="center",width="890"]

Key features of the Kanban add-on:

* *Visual Kanban board*: Integrate a fully customizable board into any Jmix view.
* *Task management*: Manage tasks within the board, including assigning them to users, setting deadlines, and priorities.
* *Stage management*: Create and manage stages (xref:flow-ui:vc/components/kanban.adoc#column[columns]) on the board, representing different phases of your workflow.
* *Drag-and-drop functionality*: Move tasks between stages with ease, providing a user-friendly and intuitive experience.
* *Customization options*: Customize the appearance and behavior of the board to suit your specific needs and preferences.
